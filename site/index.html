The problem

Autonomous money needs accountability.

Money is increasingly moved by software acting on our behalf. The controls haven't caught up.

01

No clear limits

Agents and operators can trigger payments without scoped permissions — nothing bounds what they can do.

02

No reliable proof

Financial records live in messages, screenshots, and disputed claims. "Did it happen?" has no trustless answer.

03

No clean recourse

When something goes wrong, there's no audit trail you can verify without trusting the operator.

How it works

Set the rules. Let money move.

One loop, whether the actor is a person or an agent.

01 / MESSAGE

Intent arrives

A financial message or agent intent is parsed into a structured record.

02 / POLICY

Bounds checked

Cap, counterparty, category, expiry, approval threshold — all enforced before anything moves.

03 / EVIDENCE

Stored on Walrus

The encrypted payload is stored and content-hashed as durable evidence.

04 / RECORD

Anchored on Sui

A tamper-evident record is minted on-chain — approved, rejected, or failed.

Why it matters

The actor acts. The owner controls. The chain remembers.

Autonomy without accountability is a liability. Bound makes autonomy safe — every action is permissioned, explainable, revocable, and verifiable by anyone.

Built for now and next

Same engine. Different actors. One record of truth.

FOR OPERATORS & SMEs

Books you can prove

Run financial records straight from messages, receipts, and bank alerts. Tamper-evident books for trust with partners, lenders, and auditors — no app to adopt.

FOR AI AGENTS

Wallets with limits

Give an agent a scoped policy and it can move money only within it. Over-cap or out-of-policy attempts are refused and logged. The agent can't break the rules.
